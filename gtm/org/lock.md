## THE THING THIS SHIFT LEARNED

**The brief said the serviceable fraction is where a bottom-up model quietly
becomes a top-down one. That was right, and it was not the biggest thing.**
Two choices moved the answer more than the fraction did and neither is a
multiplier — both are definitions of the noun being counted. **Locations vs
owners: 38.6% overstatement (56.5% of apparel locations belong to
multi-location firms). Employer vs nonemployer: 18.2× in barbering, 0.2× in
food.** **In a bottom-up model the arguable step is not only the fraction you
apply — it is the unit you chose before you applied anything.** A reviewer
auditing only the fractions would have passed a model already 2× adrift on its
unit. Both are now their own visible rows in the funnel.
