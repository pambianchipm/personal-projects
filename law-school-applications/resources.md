# Resources

## Official

- **LSAC** — <https://www.lsac.org> — account, LSAT registration, CAS, LOR handling, fee waivers
- **ABA Required Disclosures (509 reports)** — <https://www.abarequireddisclosures.org> — authoritative per-school data: medians, scholarship distributions, attrition, bar passage, employment
- **ABA Employment Outcomes** — each school's "employment summary" (10 months out) — the number that matters most

## Data & Prediction

- **LSData** — <https://www.lsd.law> — self-reported applicant outcomes by stats; good for gauging chances and timing
- **7Sage Admissions Predictor** — <https://7sage.com/admissions/> — probability estimates by GPA/LSAT
- **Law School Transparency** — <https://www.lawschooltransparency.com> — costs, outcomes, debt comparisons

## Essays & Process

- School admissions blogs (several deans post genuinely useful guidance during the cycle)
- Each school's own application instructions — essay prompts and page limits vary and change yearly; always pull the current year's prompt into the school's folder before drafting

## Scholarship Negotiation

- Collect competing offers before negotiating; most schools have a formal "reconsideration" process
- Check each 509 report for **conditional scholarship** stipulations (GPA/class-rank conditions) before valuing an offer

## Notes

- Prefer primary sources (school site, LSAC, ABA disclosures) over forum lore for anything that affects a decision.
