platform that happens to have AI — an employee that happens to have a platform.
That is Phin's own formulation and it is the frame.

## 3. THE FOUR EMPLOYEE PROPERTIES — ranked as the buyer experiences them

Ordered at gate r1, and the reordering is the useful part: the draft led with
*"it asks you things"* and defended it with three reasons that were **all
reasons it is good for us, none a reason it is good for her.**

1. **IT HANDS BACK FINISHED WORK.** You send a phone clip; it comes back cut and
   captioned; *"this look good?"*; your reply schedules it. **This is the demo
   that sells the tier.** It is the one property an SMB reader reacted to
   unprompted — *"I have four hundred videos on this phone and none of them are
   posted."*
2. **A REPLY IS THE APPROVAL — no app to open.** Phin's ruling. A gate called
   *"no app to open"* the strongest four words in the document.
3. **IT LIVES WHERE YOU ALREADY ARE.** In copy, name **two** surfaces, not five.
4. **IT ASKS YOU THINGS.** *"Did anyone come in about the reel?"* — and **every
   example question in copy must be answerable in one tap.** *"How did that
   discount do?"* is an essay question and is dropped. It may not lead: it is a
   cost to the buyer at the moment it happens.

**THE HONESTY RULE THAT GOVERNS ALL FOUR, and it is not negotiable at the
craft level: every layer-2 sentence is labelled as coming, with no date, or it
does not appear.** Zero of the four ship today. **A hero that sells these four
in the present tense is not a stylistic problem — it is the live breach M2 in

## 4. THE POSITION, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

> Player 2 is an AI marketing employee for your shop. It writes your posts,
> publishes them to Instagram and Facebook, runs your Meta ads, and tells you
> what worked. And nothing goes out, and nothing gets spent, without your
> approval — given per post, or armed in advance for the slots you choose.
> **If it doesn't work, you get your money back for thirty days, just by
> asking.**

**Three things about this paragraph, and the third is a hold.**

**(a) The approval qualifier is mandatory and is the reason this paragraph
survives.** *"Armed in advance for the slots you choose"* is what makes the
sentence true of a product whose premium capability is publishing without a
per-post click. **The unqualified form is barred**, and the live front door
currently ships it: `/landing` renders *"Nothing posts or spends without your
click"* eight times and `/tour` closes on *"Nothing posts or spends without your
approval"* — read on the rendered pages 2026-09-04. **My instrument here is a
regex over served markup; it counts strings, it cannot tell me whether a
visitor reads "approval" as covering a one-time arming toggle, and that is
precisely the question.**

**(b) The refund belongs next to the price, not in a footnote.** For an owner
who has already been burned by whoever last took their money, it is the reason
they risk a first month. That reader is the alternative nobody in this tree had
named, and the refund is the answer we already hold.

**(c) HELD, NOT SHIPPED — one sentence, and the class-2 pass is what stopped
it.** The r2 version of this paragraph carried ***"You're only ever charged for
images and video — never for the thinking."*** `position-v2-the-hire.md` §6.2
licenses that sentence in its may-say column. **The class-2 closed list bars
`never`/`ever` absolutes on a live surface, and the doctrine is newer than
§6.2.** I am not resolving a conflict between a ruled ban list and a ruled
doctrine by picking the one that suits the paragraph. **The metering split is
held out of the paragraph until the chief rules which governs**, and it is in
the notice as a decision, not as a rewrite. *(The sentence is also the best
short statement of the metering split we have, which is why holding it costs
something and why the hold is disclosed rather than quiet.)*

**Both gates independently named *"it isn't built yet, and we're not taking

everyone.

## 6. THE STRUCTURAL RULINGS

- **The frame leads; the guardrails follow it, visibly.** Guardrails earn the
  frame the right to say *hire* without sounding reckless. **They do not win the
  deal, and they are not a differentiator** — anyone can print an approval
  promise on a page. **What you can show is the queue.** The demo of the
  guardrail is the approval screen, not the code that enforces it.
- **Credibility first, desire second.** The platform layer's job is
  credibility. It is not the thing anyone wants.
- **Two surfaces named, never five.** A list of five reads as a roadmap.
- **The refund sits with the price.**
- **"Put the other player in" closes. It never opens.**
- **INTERNAL vocabulary never crosses to copy:** MCP surface, best-of-N,
  re-rolls, generation allowance, the metering split, "kind not quantity",
  conductor, arming, sweep, console. **"Kind, not quantity" is an internal
  framing** — an owner does not think in kinds, she thinks in what she gets and
  what it costs.
