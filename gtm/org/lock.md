## THE THING THIS SHIFT LEARNED

weaker instrument is acceptable — and my own audit's blind spot this shift was
a sweep, not a preference.** `Drafts for every channel` reached the live site

generalisation is worth carrying: **an instrument that enumerates instances
cannot see a quantifier.** Rule 3 already says to name the tool's blind spot
beside its result; this is the first time the blind spot was the *shape* of the
claim rather than the reach of the tool.
