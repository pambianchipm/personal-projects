## §2 Answer — the bank (top clusters in full; internal first, then the
press/floor-facing draft where one is safe to hold)

### O1 — bookings, not likes
**Internal:** true and known: attribution scope unaudited (truth-map
§3-bis); surfaces speak platform metrics. Never claim the bookings loop.
**Holding answer (floor/press):** "Today it proves what it can prove and
refuses to fake the rest — its reports are statistically gated, and it
publishes what it DECLINED to claim, with reasons. Bookings-level
attribution is the next read on our list, and we won't sell it before
it's real." **Address:** the attribution-scope read is the next Lane B
opening move (chief-confirmed); until then O1 stands as the top product
gap. → R8.

### O2 — zero customers
**Internal:** unanswerable by argument, by design of reality. The only
moves: concession + date + kill-criteria.
**Holding answer:** "Correct — you'd be among the first, and we say so
in writing. That's why the scan is free and shows you three posts in
your own voice before any card, why the founding rate is locked, and
why nothing posts without your click while we earn trust." For the
investor: "this raise IS the validation budget; here are the interview
dates and the kill criteria." **Address:** first real interviews (item
5, Phin's side) and ONE non-family pilot — proposed to Phin as P-A below.
**Never:** cite Dad's walkthrough as customer validation (it is a
planning/analytics receipt); never quote prompt-3 invented speech.

### O3 — what am I buying today
**Internal:** the now/later split is house discipline (⧖ labels,
operator-console-truths rule) — the answer is to PERFORM that discipline
at the counter: a one-page "works today / coming, with dates" list.
Sharp edge inside it (Journ#6): the $59 tier is LIVE while client
accounts/OAuth are Phases 1–2 away — the org needs one true sentence
about what a stranger's $59 buys this month. That sentence doesn't
exist. → P-B (Phin), R9. **Never:** roadmap verbs in present tense.

### O4 — lock-in / "your data is their moat" / mortality
**Internal:** three braided attacks. (a) Portability: NO export/
portability commitment exists anywhere in the corpus — the competitor
generator and the journalist both found the hole independently. (b) The
investor-moat vs customer-stickiness framing tension is real and on the
record in the founder's own capture. (c) Mortality: "never quits" is
already barred; downgrade.ts ("STOP AND NOTIFY… nothing may stop
SILENTLY") answers ghosting, not going-concern.
**Holding answer:** "Your accounts were always yours — your logins, your
pages. What it learns about your shop makes YOUR results better; if you
leave, your content and calendar go with you [TRUE ONLY AFTER P-C
SHIPS — do not say until then], and if we ever wind down, the system is
built to stop loudly, never silently." **Address → P-C (Phin):** a
data-export + continuity commitment (export button, wind-down policy).
→ R8/R10. The moat framing stays investor-only, reconciled as: "the
moat is that leaving costs the customer real value — which is only a
moat if staying keeps earning it; that's the same sentence."

### O5 — public AI disclosure (the round's biggest new catch)
**Internal:** the always-visibly-AI hard rule, as written, governs
owner-facing surfaces. NO artifact answers whether the PUBLISHED post or

a defensible position (owner approved it → owner's speech, like any
ghostwriter/agency), but it must be CHOSEN, on purpose, by Phin — and
whichever way, said without flinching. **Address → P-D (Phin): ruling
requested.** No press answer drafted until ruled. → R9.

### O6 — accountability & harm under standing approval
**Internal:** answer has three real legs (approval gates in code; the
