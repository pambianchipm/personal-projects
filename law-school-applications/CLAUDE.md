# Working in law-school-applications/

Conventions for AI-assisted sessions in this folder:

- **`application-tracker.md` is the source of truth** for the school list and statuses — update it whenever a deadline, status, or decision changes.
- **Never invent numbers.** Medians, deadlines, fees, and LSAT dates must come from the school's site, LSAC, or ABA 509 reports; mark anything unverified as such.
- **Essay drafts fork forward**: new numbered file per revision in `drafts/` (`v1.md`, `v2.md`, …); don't overwrite earlier versions.
- **Per-school material lives in `schools/<school-name>/`** (copied from `schools/_template/`), including the current year's actual essay prompts before any drafting.
- **This content is personal and private** — don't copy profile details, essays, or disclosures into other projects in this repo, and don't publish any of it (artifacts, gists, etc.) without being explicitly asked.
- The applicant's profile is in `profile/profile.md`; read it before giving school-list or essay advice.
