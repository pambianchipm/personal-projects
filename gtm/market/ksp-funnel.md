## 1. Laundry list — via the five prompts (deck p23, verbatim prompts)

### Prompt 1 — "Tell me in three bullets why someone should buy your product."

- Does your marketing so you can run the shop
- Costs a fraction of a typical hire
- Nothing posts or spends without your OK

### Prompt 2 — "What is your special sauce? The thing you do that's unique, or better than what others do?"

- Runs the whole loop: plan, write, post, learn
- Tells the truth about what worked (claims engine)
- Refuses to overclaim its own results
- Every post is its own experiment
- Winners get budget, losers get cut
- An employee you can talk to, not a dashboard
- Never posts silently-broken; alerts you instead
- Shows three posts in your voice before any card

### Prompt 3 — "Fast forward a few years. You have a customer who is telling a peer why they chose your product. What things do they list?"

*(LAW-1 LABEL ON THE WHOLE SECTION: every bullet below is INVENTED future
customer speech — the deck's own exercise format. Search directions for
concept endlines; never evidence, never quotable as an owner's words.)*

- "I put my phone down on Saturday" *(the landscape report's synthesized
  candidate line — a message to TEST, never evidence; positioning.md §9)*
- "It just handles it; I tap approve"
- "It knows my shop's voice by now"
- "The Monday email runs my whole week" ⧖
- "It asks for photos before I forget events" ⧖
- "When something flops, it says so"
- "Cheaper than the kid I almost hired"
- "I never wonder if posting actually happened"

### Prompt 4 — "What things are a competitive advantage for you now that will still be an advantage in 5, 10 years?"

- Approval gates in the architecture, not the marketing
- Honesty machinery: suppressed claims persisted with reasons
- Outcome memory compounding per shop over years
- The employee frame itself (category, not feature)
- Cost structure: software margins under service-band prices
- Trust from always being visibly AI

### Prompt 5 — "What things are true about your product or service philosophy that you intend to retain no matter what happens with the product suite and company path?"

- The owner's click gates every post and dollar
- Never pretend; an empty week is an empty week
- Always visibly AI, never a fake human
- The employee works around a busy boss ⧖
- Silence over noise ("nothing new today" is honored)
- Escalate to a human when stuck ⧖
