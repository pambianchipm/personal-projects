# Cycle Timeline — Applying Fall 2026 for Fall 2027 Matriculation

Working backward from the goal: **applications submitted by late October / early November 2026**.
Rolling admissions means earlier ≈ better odds and better scholarship money.

> Dates below are approximate — confirm current LSAT administration dates and each school's
> open/deadline dates on [lsac.org](https://www.lsac.org) and the schools' sites.

## Now → September 2026

- [ ] Create / update **LSAC account** and register for **Credential Assembly Service (CAS)**
- [ ] Request **transcripts** sent to LSAC from every institution attended (this takes weeks — start now)
- [ ] **LSAT**: if not done, register (administrations run roughly monthly — Aug/Sept/Oct/Nov; register ~6 weeks out). An October score still comfortably makes this cycle; November is workable but late for rolling.
- [ ] Ask **recommenders** now (see `recommendations/recommenders.md`) — give them 4–6 weeks and a packet (resume, transcript, talking points)
- [ ] Fill in `profile/profile.md` and build the first-pass school list
- [ ] Personal statement: brainstorm → outline → first draft

## September – October 2026

- [ ] Applications open (most schools ~Sept 1–15) — start filling them in as they open
- [ ] Finalize **personal statement** (2–4 serious revision rounds; get outside readers)
- [ ] Draft **school-specific essays** ("Why X", optionals) and any **addenda**
- [ ] Finalize **resume** (law school version: 1 page, more detail on impact than a job resume)
- [ ] Confirm all LORs are **submitted to LSAC**
- [ ] Request **fee waivers** where available (many schools grant them just for asking / from CAS data)

## October – November 2026

- [ ] **Submit** — target the bulk of applications by end of October; everything by Thanksgiving
- [ ] Verify each application went **complete** (schools mark complete only once CAS report + LORs arrive)

## December 2026 – April 2027

- [ ] Interviews (some schools: Kira video interviews or alumni interviews — prep in school folders)
- [ ] Decisions arrive; log them in `application-tracker.md`
- [ ] **Scholarship negotiation** window (competing offers are leverage — this is normal and expected)
- [ ] Seat deposits typically due **April–May 2027**

## Key dependencies (slowest first)

1. **Recommenders** — human latency; ask earliest
2. **Transcripts to LSAC** — institutional latency, weeks
3. **LSAT score** — fixed administration + ~3-week score release
4. **Personal statement** — quality needs calendar time, not just effort
