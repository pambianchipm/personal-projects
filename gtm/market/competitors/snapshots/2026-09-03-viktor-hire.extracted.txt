Q: What work can I delegate to Viktor?
A: Delegate recurring work and one-off projects: morning briefings, reports, dashboard updates, CRM hygiene, ad audits, outbound research, apps, and code. Viktor works across 3,200+ tools and delivers the finished output in Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Q: How quickly can my team start?
A: Install Viktor in Slack or Microsoft Teams, connect your tools through OAuth, and delegate the first task in minutes. You do not have to build a workflow or train your team on a new app.

Q: How much does an AI employee cost?
A: Viktor starts free with $100 in credits, every feature, and every integration. No credit card is required. Paid plans start at $50 per month for the whole workspace, not per seat.

Q: Can my whole team share one AI employee?
A: Yes. Viktor works in shared Slack or Microsoft Teams channels, keeps context for the team, and lets anyone delegate work. One hire can support every department.

Q: Is Viktor secure?
A: Viktor is SOC 2 compliant, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and never trains on your data. Each team gets an isolated compute environment, and sensitive actions wait for your approval.
