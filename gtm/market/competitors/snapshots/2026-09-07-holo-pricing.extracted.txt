Questions, answered
What is Holo?
Holo is your all-in-one AI marketing tool. It creates ads, social posts, and emails that sound and look like they came from your team. No templates. No generic fluff. Just brand-perfect campaigns, ready to launch.
How is this AI tool for advertising different from others?
Holo was built for real marketing performance - not generic outputs. Here’s how it stands apart:
1. Brand DNA, Built In
Holo learns your tone, style, and audience - so every campaign looks and feels like it came from your team.
2. Proven Ad Intelligence
Trained on 10M+ creative assets and 19,000+ top-performing ads, Holo generates campaigns designed to convert, not just fill space.
3. Global-Ready 95+ languages supported. Launch brand-consistent campaigns for any market, anywhere in the world.
Do I need marketing or technical knowledge to use Holo?
No. Holo was built for founders, creators, and marketers who want speed and results - not a learning curve.
What kind of content can Holo generate?
Everything you need to grow your business:
• Ad creatives (static and video)
• Social media posts
• Email sequences
• Promotional campaigns
And more - always aligned with your voice.
Can I manage multiple brands in one account?
Yes. Run up to 5 brands under a single Holo account. Each with its own Brand DNA, workspace, and templates.
Do I need design or editing skills to use the AI ad generator?
No. Holo handles the creative. You focus on the launch.
Is Holo better than ChatGPT?
They’re powerful general AI tools. But Holo is built for one thing: marketing.
With ChatGPT or Sora, you’re stuck in back-and-forth prompts, tweaking outputs, and piecing together visuals that don’t match your brand. Holo eliminates all of that. It’s a dedicated AI ad generator - your campaigns are created, branded, and ready to launch in minutes. No prompt engineering. No guesswork. Just high-performing marketing assets, done.
Do you have a free trial?
No. Holo doesn't offer a free trial. You can request a refund within 7 calendar days of a payment if none of the credits from that payment were used.
Is Holo suitable for my business?
Yes, Holo is suitable for any business. It’s especially effective for product-based brands that want to create high-quality content quickly and consistently.
Does it work if I don't have a website?
Yes. You can use any valid URL during onboarding and update your brand information later in the process.
