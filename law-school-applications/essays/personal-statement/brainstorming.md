# Personal Statement — Brainstorming

The goal: a true story only you could tell, that leaves the reader thinking
"this person will be good at law school and knows why they're going."

## Prompts to mine

- A moment you changed your mind about something important
- The hardest problem you've worked on and what it revealed about how you think
- When did "law school" go from idea to decision? What actually happened?
- What do you do that other applicants in your position don't?
- What would the people who know you best say is your defining trait — and what story shows it?

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Resume-in-prose (the resume already exists)
- "I've wanted to be a lawyer since childhood" openings
- Trauma without reflection, or accomplishment without stakes
- Writing about the law in the abstract instead of yourself in particular
- Spending the last paragraph on the school (that's the "Why X" essay's job)
