# 5 B2C MLP Ideas — GTM-First Playbook

Profile these were built around: solo full-stack dev with design/product strength, full-time,
<$5k budget, no existing audience. Channels you said you'd grind on: **short-form video,
SEO/content, X/LinkedIn**. Competition is treated as validation; each idea includes the niche
wedge to differentiate into later.

The filter used for every idea:
1. MLP shippable solo in 2–4 weeks
2. The product **demos well in under 15 seconds on video** and/or has a **free-tool SEO wedge**
3. Monetization works from day one without paid acquisition
4. Validated category (competitors making money), with room for a niche angle

---

## 1. Progress-Timelapse Tracker (TikTok-first, viral loop built in)

**What:** A mobile app for one visual-progress niche — e.g., gym progress photos, art
improvement, home renovation, hair growth — that turns your photo history into a beautiful,
shareable timelapse/"year in review" card.

**Why it fits you:** The share artifact IS the marketing. Every user who posts their timelapse
is a free ad with your watermark. This is the same loop that made Strava, BeReal recaps, and
Spotify Wrapped work.

**MLP scope (3 weeks):** Photo capture with alignment ghost-overlay, timeline view,
timelapse/collage export with subtle branding, reminders. Cut: social features, AI analysis,
Android (ship iOS first).

**GTM playbook:**
- Primary: TikTok/Reels — post your own transformation timelapse daily + duet/stitch
  transformation content (one of the highest-performing formats that exists)
- Loop: export watermark + "made with X" → App Store search
- Secondary: build in public on X (devlog of the app itself)

**Monetization:** Free 1 project / paywall unlimited + HD export. $20–30/yr or $4/mo.

**Validation:** HabitKit (solo dev, six figures), Gridy, progress-pic apps with poor UX and
strong revenue. Wedge = pick ONE niche (e.g., lifting) and own its content style.

## 2. Wedding Speech & Toast Studio (SEO + TikTok, one-time pricing)

**What:** A guided web app that interviews you (chat-style) and drafts a best-man / maid-of-honor
/ father-of-the-bride speech, then helps you rehearse (teleprompter mode, timing, laugh beats).

**Why it fits you:** Massive evergreen long-tail SEO ("best man speech examples brother",
"maid of honor speech for sister funny") + wedding content is a top TikTok category. High-stakes
moment = real willingness to pay. One-time purchase avoids subscription fatigue and churn math.

**MLP scope (2–3 weeks):** Landing + 30–50 SEO template/example pages, guided intake form,
LLM draft + revision loop, teleprompter view, Stripe one-time checkout. Cut: accounts (magic
link), mobile app, other speech types (add graduation/retirement later — same engine).

**GTM playbook:**
- Primary: programmatic SEO — one page per speech-type × relationship × tone; free examples,
  paid personalization
- Secondary: TikTok "rate this best man speech" / before-after speech glow-ups; wedding
  hashtags are enormous
- Seasonal spike: engagement season (Dec–Feb) → wedding season (May–Oct)

**Monetization:** $29–49 one-time per speech. High margin, impulse-purchasable, giftable.

**Validation:** Provenance.co, WeddingSpeech.ai, Joy's tools — plus decades of "speech writing
services" on Fiverr. Wedge = the rehearsal/delivery experience nobody else does well.

## 3. Carousel & Repurposing Studio for X/LinkedIn creators (audience = customers)

**What:** Paste a blog post, thread, or transcript → get designed LinkedIn carousels, X thread
variants, and short-form video scripts with hook options. Opinionated templates that don't look
like Canva.

**Why it fits you:** You're about to grind X/LinkedIn anyway — with this idea, **using your own
product creates your distribution**. Every carousel you post is a product demo. Prosumer-B2C
pricing, B2B-adjacent buyers (they expense it).

**MLP scope (2 weeks):** 5–8 excellent carousel templates, LLM chunking of long-form → slides,
PDF/PNG export, hook generator. Cut: scheduling/posting APIs, analytics, teams.

**GTM playbook:**
- Primary: build in public on LinkedIn/X using only your own tool; "I made this carousel in
  90 seconds" meta-content
- Secondary: free tool wedge — "LinkedIn carousel maker" free tier ranks + converts
- Tertiary: TikTok "watch me turn this article into a carousel" screen-capture content

**Monetization:** $12–19/mo. Free = 3 exports/mo with watermark.

**Validation:** aiCarousels, Taplio ($M+ ARR), ContentDrips. Wedge = design quality + speed;
most competitors output generic-looking slides.

## 4. Photo Declutter Swipe App ("Tinder for your camera roll")

**What:** Swipe left to delete, right to keep; monthly "clean your January photos" sessions;
storage-saved counter; duplicate/screenshot detection.

**Why it fits you:** The single best format-fit for short-form video of any idea here — the
demo is 8 seconds, universally relatable ("47GB of screenshots??"), and satisfying to watch.
Pure cold-start-friendly: no audience needed, the content concept carries it.

**MLP scope (2–3 weeks, React Native/Expo or Swift):** Swipe UI, month-by-month sessions,
delete confirmation batch, storage counter, streaks. Cut: AI similarity clustering (v2),
cloud anything.

**GTM playbook:**
- Primary: TikTok — "cleaning 10 years of camera roll" POV content, satisfying
  before/after storage numbers; comment-section engagement is the engine
- Secondary: App Store Optimization ("delete photos", "clean storage" — high-volume terms)
- This category is *proven* to grow purely on TikTok organic

**Monetization:** $3–5/wk or $20–30/yr subscription, hard paywall after first session
(this category converts well on aggressive paywalls).

**Validation:** SwipeWipe, Slidebox, CleanUp apps — several at $50k+ MRR, mostly grown via
TikTok. Wedge = better session design (monthly memories angle) + genuinely good free tier
to farm reviews.

## 5. Freelancer Money Toolkit (programmatic-SEO calculator wedge)

**What:** Free calculators — freelance rate calculator, quarterly tax estimator,
salary-vs-freelance comparison, invoice generator — funneling into a paid "money OS for
freelancers": income smoothing view, tax set-aside autopilot math, rate benchmarks.

**Why it fits you:** Calculators are the most reliable programmatic SEO wedge that exists —
thousands of long-tail queries ("freelance rate calculator UX designer", "1099 tax calculator
Texas"), low competition per page, compounding traffic. Also strong LinkedIn content surface
(freelancing/solopreneur content performs).

**MLP scope (3–4 weeks):** 10–20 calculator pages (one template, parameterized), email capture
("save your results"), one paid feature to start: the personalized tax set-aside + rate report.
Cut: bank connections/Plaid (v2 — it's the moat later, not the MLP).

**GTM playbook:**
- Primary: programmatic SEO on calculators; each state/profession combo is a page
- Secondary: LinkedIn/X posts with the data ("what 500 freelancers' rates look like") —
  the tool generates its own content
- Tertiary: short-form "how much should you charge" calculators-on-camera

**Monetization:** Free calculators → $8–12/mo or $79/yr for the toolkit. Affiliate revenue
(accounting software, business banking) monetizes non-converters.

**Validation:** Keeper Tax, FlyFin, Found grew this way; SmartAsset built a company on
calculator SEO. Wedge = one profession first (e.g., freelance designers/devs) with real
rate benchmarks.

## How to choose (recommendation)

- **Fastest to signal (2–4 weeks to real data):** #4 Photo Declutter — the TikTok content
  either hits or it doesn't, and you'll know fast.
- **Best compounding asset:** #5 Freelancer Toolkit — SEO takes 2–3 months to kick in but
  keeps paying; slowest feedback loop though.
- **Best strategic fit if you want to build an audience while building the product:** #3
  Carousel Studio — dogfooding = distribution.
- **Highest willingness-to-pay per user:** #2 Wedding Speech — one-time $39 from cold SEO
  traffic is a beautiful bootstrap motion.
- **Biggest upside if the loop works:** #1 Progress Timelapse.

A sane sequencing: pick ONE, timebox 2 weeks build + 4 weeks GTM grind, define a kill/continue
metric up front (e.g., 1 video >50k views, or 20 organic signups/day, or $500 revenue), and
hold yourself to it.

## The 30-day GTM cadence (applies to whichever you pick)

- **Week 0 (pre-launch):** Landing page + waitlist. Start posting daily in your channel
  *before* the product exists — validate the content angle first; it's the cheapest test.
- **Weeks 1–2 (build):** Devlog build-in-public on X. 1 TikTok/day testing hooks for the
  product's core "moment."
- **Weeks 3–4 (launch):** Product Hunt + relevant subreddits (as a user, not a marketer),
  2 short-form videos/day iterating on whichever hook performed, ship SEO pages if the idea
  has them.
- **Daily non-negotiable:** 1 piece of content + 10 genuine replies/comments in places your
  users hang out. Consistency beats cleverness on every one of these channels.

**Metric that matters:** not followers — *activation events from content* (installs, signups,
checkouts attributable to a post). Track from day one.
