# Letter of Recommendation Tracker

Most schools want 2 letters (some accept 3–4). Academic letters carry the most weight for
recent grads; work-supervisor letters matter more the longer you've been out of school.
Letters go to **LSAC** once and get assigned per-school from there.

## The ask (checklist per recommender)

- [ ] Ask in person / by call if possible; email as fallback — and ask if they can write a **strong** letter
- [ ] Send a packet: resume, transcript, deadline, 3–5 bullet points of things they could speak to
- [ ] Enter them in LSAC so they get the upload link
- [ ] Gentle nudge at 2 weeks if not submitted; letters are the #1 source of last-minute delays
- [ ] Thank-you note after submission; update them when decisions land

## Draft ask email

> Subject: Letter of recommendation — law school
>
> Hi <name>, I'm applying to law school this fall and I'm hoping you'd be willing to write me
> a strong letter of recommendation. [One sentence on why them specifically.] The letter goes
> to a central service (LSAC) once, by <date>. I'll send a short packet with my resume and
> some reminders of our work together. Totally understand if your schedule doesn't allow it —
> and thank you either way.
