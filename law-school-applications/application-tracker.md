## Reminders

- Most schools admit **rolling** — applying in September/October is materially better than December, even before the "deadline."
- Early Decision is **binding** at most schools; only use it where you'd unconditionally attend.
- Medians move year to year — pull current ones from each school's ABA 509 report (see `resources.md`).
