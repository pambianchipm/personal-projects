## 0. Before you start

- **Who:** 5–8 owner-operators of local visual businesses (salon, gym/
  studio, cafe/restaurant, boutique), no marketing hire. Recruit pool =
  bootstrap decision 5 (still open — scanned-leads pipeline vs separate
  list; your call, but leads who KNOW Player 2 exists are slightly
  contaminated for the frame test; note who knew).
- **Session:** ~30 min. Print the four cards + this kit's sheets.
- **The bias warning, honestly (deck p20):** the method recommends a
  neutral interviewer because founders hear what they hope. You're
  running these yourself, so the mitigations are mandatory: read cards
  VERBATIM, never explain or rescue a card, let silences sit, never
  answer an objection during the session ("great question — what would
  YOU want it to do?"), and write down the words they use, not what you
  understood them to mean.
- **Roadmap ledger — memorize this:** if asked directly "does it do X
  today?": card R's "you hear about it first" alerting is built but
  reaches the operator, not yet the client — and the SAME limit applies
  to the feature table's "Posting that never fails silently" row (the
  flag reaches the operator today; "so I'd get told?" gets the honest
  line, not a yes); the feature list's items marked (coming) are
  approved builds, not shipped. The honest line:
  "that part's coming — here's what works today." NEVER affirm an
  unshipped capability as present; the cards are research stimuli, not
  promises, and you may say exactly that.
- Ground rules script: "This is confidential, and I'm looking for your
  honest reaction — you cannot hurt my feelings; the blunter you are
  the more useful this is. The product name on these pages is made up.
  Nothing here is a sales pitch."

## 1. Grounding (≈7 min) — needs first, cards later

Get them talking about THEIR world before any stimulus:

1. "Walk me through how marketing happens at your shop today. Who does
   it, when, what does it cost you?"
2. "What have you tried — apps, freelancers, agencies, nephews? What
   happened?"
3. **THE AXIS-F UNPROMPTED PROBE (ask before any card, word-for-word):**
   "If you could know ONE thing about whether your marketing is
   working, what would you want to know?" → capture sheet: did they
   name bookings / customers / dollars UNPROMPTED (Y/N) and their exact
   words. Do not suggest options. This tests the org's biggest open
   question — whether results-in-owner-terms is really the first ask.
4. **RELIEF vs GROWTH, open form:** "Imagine the marketing was somehow
   handled. What's different for you?" → capture whether their answer
   is time/relief-shaped ("my Sunday back") or growth-shaped ("more
   people in"). Their words, verbatim. Never say either option first.
5. The moments question (truth-map §9): "I'll describe a few moments —
   tell me which, if any, would make this feel like STAFF rather than
   SOFTWARE: (a) Monday morning an email arrives — your week's
   marketing, planned; you tap once to approve. (b) It texts you:
   'anything I should know about the holiday weekend? Send a photo if
   you have one.' (c) A monthly report that says plainly 'this worked,
   this didn't.' (d) You message it 'we're closed Monday' and it fixes
   the week itself." → rank/reactions verbatim.

## 2. Concept review (≈15 min)

**Rotation (F1 symmetry — mandatory):** M and K must not always
lead/follow each other in the same order, and neither is ever
introduced with enthusiasm. Schedule across interviews:

| Interview | Order |
|---|---|
| 1 | M · T · K · R |
| 2 | K · R · M · T |
| 3 | T · M · R · K |
| 4 | R · K · T · M |
| 5+ | repeat cycle |

Per card (deck p20, verbatim ritual): read the card ALOUD, hand it
over, then: "Write the main idea in your own words at the top. Circle
anything you like. Cross out anything you don't. Underline anything
confusing. Then give it a 1–5." Then discuss what they marked — why,
in their words.

**Card-specific probes (after the markup, never before):**
- K's "watches what's working in your kind of business": "What do you
  picture it actually watching?" (M10 nouns test — do they imagine
  competitor stalking, trends, their own street? Note if they'd want
  the salon-next-door version, and note THAT expectation for the O18
  conflict question.)
- M's reporting line (probe symmetry with K's): "You circled / crossed
  'won't pretend when nothing did' — say more?"
- R's trust framing: "You crossed out / circled 'trust with the keys' —
  say more?" If lock-in worry appears anywhere ("can I get out?"),
  probe gently: "what would make leaving feel safe?" (O4/P-C evidence.)
- After the last card: "Rank the four. What would the top one have to
  PROVE for you to pay for it?" (their proof bar, verbatim — feeds the
  RTB ordering.)
- Frame check, last: "Some people would call this an app, some would
  call it more like hiring someone. Which is it to you, and does the
  price change depending on which it is?" (the folder test, said
  plainly — capture the folder AND the price reaction.)
- Dial cadence (only if time): "If this thing could contact you — how
  often is helpful, and where's annoying? Twice a week? Five times?"

## 3. Feature-list review (≈8 min) — deck p20 format

Hand them this table: "Score each 1–5 on how much it MATTERS to you,
and 1–5 on whether it feels DIFFERENT from what's out there. Then pick
your top three."

| Feature | What it means | Matters 1–5 | Different 1–5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free preview before paying | It reads your public pages and writes three posts in your voice before any card | | |
| Your OK gates everything | Nothing posts or spends money without your approval — per post, or approve the pattern once | | |
| Honest monthly report | Says what worked; won't pretend when nothing did | | |

| Learns your shop | Keeps what worked, cuts what didn't, gets sharper over time | | |

## 4. Capture sheet (one per interview — this is the evidence of record)

- Interviewee #, business type, knew-of-Player-2? (Y/N), date.
- AXIS-F unprompted: Y/N + verbatim words.
- Relief-vs-growth open answer: verbatim.
- Moments ranking (a–d) + words.
- Card scores: M __ K __ T __ R __ · rank order · main-idea-in-their-
  words per card (staple the marked cards to this sheet).
- Proof bar ("what would it have to prove"): verbatim.
- Folder answer (app vs hire) + price reaction: verbatim.
- Lock-in signals, M10 expectations, dial cadence: verbatim if raised.
- The one sentence you'd put on a billboard from this interview:
  their words, not yours.

## 5. What happens with the results

Scores aggregate by code letter across interviews (deck p10 — that's
what the letters are for). The org's merge/narrowing (M-list → final
3–5 KSPs), the frame A/B decision's first real evidence, F1's
arbitration, and benefit-wording rewrites all consume these sheets.
File them (or photos of them) to the chief; the boss folds them in
next Lane B shift. n=5–8 is signal, not proof — the org will grade
findings accordingly (no over-claiming a five-interview result; law 1
applies to us too).
