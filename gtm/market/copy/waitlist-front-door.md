**Why it survived four gates when the rest of the file did not.** Both
personas, independently, named *"it isn't built yet, and we're not taking
money for it"* the strongest line in the document. The SMB owner: *"that's the
most honest sentence anyone has said to me in this category and I'd give you
my email for it."*

**What was cut from the draft at r1, and must not come back** — each was a
promise the product cannot keep:
- *"where you already text"* — iMessage by paraphrase. The shipped surface is
  not iMessage. Say **"the chat apps you already use"** and no more.
- *"send back finished video"* — promises a loop whose feasibility spike has
  not run.
- *"you'll be first, at a founding rate"* — implies a price for a tier that is
  explicitly not priced, and an ordering promise an operator-set cohort field
  cannot honour for everyone.
